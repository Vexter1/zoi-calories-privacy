Zoi Health Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

The short version

Your food log, goals, weight and settings are stored only on your device. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking, and we don't collect or sell your personal data.

What the app stores on your device

Your name (if you add one), calorie and macro goals, food log, favourites, custom foods, weight entries, the details you enter in the calorie calculator, your saved meals, water intake, your reminder times and your appearance and unit choices. This stays on your phone. If you delete the app or reset your data, it is gone, so use Export to keep a backup.

Food searches and barcodes

When you search for a food online or scan a barcode, the search words or barcode number are sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to find the product. Like any website, they can see your IP address. Nothing else from your log or profile is sent. Open Food Facts has its own privacy policy.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to scan barcodes, and scans aren't saved. Photo scanning of meals is not available at the moment. If it is added later, this page will be updated before it is switched on.

Notifications

Meal and water reminders are scheduled on your phone and are never sent from a server. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings at any time.

Your choices

You can export your data, restore it, or delete everything from Settings at any time. Because your data lives only on your device, deleting the app also removes it.

Age

Zoi Health is intended for people aged 18 and over.

Health information

Calorie and nutrition figures are estimates and the app is not medical advice. Speak to your GP before making big changes to how you eat.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change and the new version will be in the app. Questions or requests: zoicaloriessupport@gmail.com.
